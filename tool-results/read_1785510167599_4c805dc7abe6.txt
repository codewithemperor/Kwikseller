   160→   160→### How the layout is wired (investigation)
   161→   161→
   162→   162→The root layout (`src/app/layout.tsx`) wraps **every** route in
   163→   163→`<MarketplaceLayout>{children}</MarketplaceLayout>` (inside Suspense +
   164→   164→providers). `MarketplaceLayout` is a client component that already conditionally
   165→   165→hides its header (`hideTopNav`) for cart/auth/vendor-storefront routes, and
   166→   166→conditionally hides ALL chrome (footer, mobile nav, floating widgets) for vendor
   167→   167→storefront routes (`isVendorStorefrontRoute`). The `(auth)` route group has its
   168→   168→own layout but is still wrapped by MarketplaceLayout — the header is just hidden
   169→   169→via the `hideTopNav` flag.
   170→   170→
   171→   171→This means a route-group `(account)/layout.tsx` alone would NOT suffice — the
   172→   172→root-level MarketplaceLayout would still render its header/footer around the
   173→   173→AccountLayout. The MarketplaceLayout MUST be taught to skip its chrome for
   174→   174→account routes. Given that constraint, per-page wrapping is the safest approach
   175→   175→(no folder moves, no import-path risk, no route-segment conflict between
   176→   176→`(account)/orders/page.tsx` and `orders/[id]/page.tsx`).
